## Implementation: route-specific low-output diagnostics, not a speculative MMU semantic change

The production TLB synchronization path in `pcsx2/COP0.cpp:SyncActiveTLBMappings` has 3 exclusive outcomes: (1) compact 48-slot memo hit/early return; (2) bounded <=64-page delta; (3) full projection map build and diff. The existing v53 summary timed all routes together. Its large total cannot distinguish a cheap high-frequency memo hit from costly full rebuild or host mapping work.

Changes, opt-in only under the **existing** `[EmuCore] EEPerformanceProbe = true` setting:
1. `pcsx2/EEPerformanceProbe.h`: atomic route-specific timing `tlbMemoHits`, `tlbSmallDeltas`, `tlbFullRebuilds`, plus counts for ASID cause, small delta candidate pages, removed/installed/permission-only mappings, unchanged-or-retained mappings. New `RecordTLBSync()` captures one duration and credits both the old total and **exactly one** route to preserve comparable totals.
2. `pcsx2/COP0.cpp`: tag memo-hit early return, bounded-delta path, or default full-rebuild path; pass existing semantic counters to the timing helper at `finishSync()`. No changes to architecture-visible TLB state, mapping actions, invalidation policy, or guest instruction execution.
3. `pcsx2/x86/ix86-32/iR5900.cpp`: at shutdown emit one extra low-volume `EE-PERF-TLB-ROUTES` summary (together with existing `EE-PERF-SUMMARY`); no per-event spam.
4. `tests/ctest/core/ee_rec_code_cache_tests_20261020.cpp`: verify total and disjoint route counts, aggregate times, ASID and mapping counters, reset behavior.

**Note:** the `unchangedOrRetained` count combines unchanged mapping callbacks plus all pages retained on a memo hit, which can heavily inflate the number; interpret `removed`, `installed`, `permissionOnly` separately. The timer includes the full sync path until `finishSync()`, so mapping effects are included; route timing fields sum to approximately the old `tlbSyncUs` (integer microsecond truncation may introduce a small difference). Instrumentation adds a few atomic increments when enabled and is not itself a speed fix.
